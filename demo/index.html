FAQ

Questions, answered

Straight answers about insurance, warranty and service.

Do you work with my insurance company?

Yes. We work with all insurance companies and have a Direct Repair Program with most major insurers. We contact your insurer and work on your behalf.

Is there a warranty on the repair?

A1 Body and Glass guarantees all work against defects in workmanship and materials for as long as you own your vehicle. Coverage applies to the repairs listed on your invoice; some exclusions apply, see Our Work.

Can you pick up and deliver my vehicle?

Yes. We can pick up your vehicle and deliver it when repairs are complete. We also have a courtesy van, and Enterprise rental is available.

Do you tow disabled vehicles?

Yes, from anywhere, with flatbed and wheel lift. Towing is provided by A1 Body and Glass, LLC (Broward County License # TI-00166).

Do you repair fleet and commercial vehicles?

Yes. Our Commercial Division, A1 Truck & RV Center, is at 1982 NW 44th Street in Pompano Beach.

Can I follow my repair online?

Yes, with the Repair Status link on our site.
